there are two key reasons why spotify is so convenient

1. all the music
2. all the time

The first reason spotify is indespensable is the fact that you can stream any song. If this is an indespensible part of your listening habits, at least switch to TIDAL. TIDAL is the same subscription price as spotify and pays artists 3x more on average. Otherwise, if you only want to stream occasionally when searching for songs, youtube provides this service. if ads are an issue, use an adblocker. the positives of using an adblocker far outweigh the negatives to artists if it is the crucial factor preventing you from switching over.

The second resaon is the fact that you can stream music from anywhere. pull it up on your laptop, or your phone. download songs (*you still don't own them*). if this is a babarrier, you can set up an on-the-go music library and play it from anywhere. this can be overcome but the process is a bit more complicated. i've described how i go about make this work below.

## source

have a folder/music library on your computer. this is your master source of music. remember to back this up from time to time. and this is where you buy music

buy a nice fast usb flash drive, preferably with both a type-a and type-c end. this is your portable music collection (re(c|k)ord box), equivalent to the songs you would otherwise have on spotify.

## player

if you can find an old stereo that supports usb - or maybe aready have one, then set this up as your main music player. your car likely already has a stereo that supports usb as well.

for 'streaming' on the go, find a cheap android phone with a headphone jack and usb-c (this is the hard part, but getting easier). this is your music player. if your usb only has a usb a end you can buy a something like [this](https://www.techexpress.co.nz/products/angled-usb-c-to-usb-31-type-a-female-otg-adapter) but one with both type-a and type-c ends is ideal. bonus points for moving your sim card to that phone and not installing any other apps for a healthier daily driver. now you essentially have a spotify-like experience on your phone, but you own your music and it will never disappear.

> note: this will not work with any iphone due to lack of USB-OTG protocol.
